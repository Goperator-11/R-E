[5] Why burstiness matters: same loss rate, different burst length
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    K = 64, p = 2.0%, T_recover = 80 ms

  burst L   r*  overhead   P_fail(r*)   P_fail with r* from L=1
        1    5       8%     1.97e-03                  1.97e-03
        2   10      16%     3.79e-03                  4.80e-02
        4   14      22%     9.99e-03                  8.84e-02
        8   18      28%     1.85e-02                  9.03e-02
       16   15      23%     3.81e-02                  7.04e-02

  -> assuming independent losses fixes r at 5; the last column shows
     the real failure probability that choice leaves you with.

    interleaving (D) breaks the burst apart instead of paying for it:
      burst L   D   r*  overhead      P_fail
            8   1   18      28%    1.85e-02
            8   4   11      17%    5.41e-03
            8   8    8      12%    3.24e-03
           16   1   15      23%    3.81e-02
           16   4   15      23%    1.01e-02
           16   8   11      17%    5.98e-03

    -> D>1 costs latency (you must span D blocks), not bandwidth.
       Trading latency for redundancy is the cheaper deal on bursty links.
